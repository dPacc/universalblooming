**Key nuance for copy.** "Nursery" (Arabic *hadana*) under federal law and in ADEK usage means **birth/45 days to 4 years**. Dubai's KHDA "Early Childhood Centre" licence allows **45 days to 6 years**, which covers FS1–FS2/KG and up to Year 1/KG2. That is why some Dubai ECCs run KG classes.

## 2. Admission age / cut-off rules (for the calculator)

### 2.1 The national rule from AY 2026-27 (MoE, announced 17 Dec 2025)
Source: https://www.moe.gov.ae/En/MediaCenter/News/Pages/UAE-announces-updated-age-cut-off-date-for-KG-Grade-1-admissions-starting-AY-2026-2027.aspx **[OFFICIAL]**. Corroborated by Khaleej Times (https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/education/uae-announces-new-age-cut-off-for-kg-grade-1-school-admissions-from-next-academic-year) **[MEDIA]**.

- For all schools and kindergartens whose year starts in **August/September**, the cut-off moves from **31 August** to **31 December of the admission year**.
- **April-start schools** keep **31 March**, which is unchanged.
- The rule applies to **new admissions only**. Currently enrolled pupils are unaffected. Transfers and arrivals from abroad are placed by the last completed grade.

### 2.2 Master table: September-start schools (all emirates, from 2026-27)
The child must have **completed** the age **on or before 31 December of the admission year**. That means their birthday falls on or before 31 Dec.

Sources: the MoE release above [OFFICIAL]; the KHDA Student Placement Guidelines FAQ (Parents), Appendix 1 "Comparative Age and Curriculum Equivalence Table" (PDF dated 24 Jun 2026) at https://web.khda.gov.ae/KHDA/media/KHDA/FAQs-Parent_SPG.pdf [OFFICIAL, read in full].

- Calculator formula for a September-start school: `age_at(Dec 31 of the academic year's start year) >= required_age`.

### 2.6 Previous rule (for "what changed" content)
Before 2026-27, September-start schools used a **31 August** cut-off (FS1 = 3 by 31 Aug), under Ministerial Resolution No. 24 of 2021 on age requirements. The KHDA FAQ still lists this resolution as governing, as amended by the MoE "Admission Age Guide for KG and Grade 1 Students", December 2025. Status: [OFFICIAL].

### 2.7 Nursery (pre-FS1) ages
There is no "grade" before Pre-KG. Under-3s join nursery rooms by age: typically **45 days** at the earliest in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Federal law covers children up to 4 years; KHDA ECCs cover up to 6 years. Room names such as "Babies", "Toddlers" and "Pre-FS1" are operator conventions, not regulated terms. Status: [OFFICIAL] for the age limits; [UNCERTAIN] for room naming.

## 4. Registration documents

**Official (Abu Dhabi, ADEK)** from https://adek.gov.ae/en/Education-System/Nurseries/Registration-Requirements **[OFFICIAL]**:
1. Completed registration form (child's name, nationality, date of birth, enrolment date, address, phone numbers)
2. Copy of the Emirates ID card
3. Copy of the child's birth certificate
4. The child's original Emirates ID, shown at registration
5. Any other documents the nursery requests

Registration is done directly with the nursery; parents do not need to go through ADEK. The child must be **under 4**. Gulf News adds: original Emirates IDs of the parents, and that nurseries may ask for a tenancy contract or immunisation record [MEDIA].

**Al Ghad (public, Abu Dhabi)** additionally needs parents' Emirates IDs, proof of residence, marital status, an employer letter (for working mothers) and a People of Determination card if applicable [OFFICIAL].

**Typical Dubai/UAE nursery checklist** (operator guides; [UNCERTAIN] as a rule, but consistent across sources):
- Child's passport copy and UAE residence visa (or Emirates ID now used in place of a visa sticker)
- Child's Emirates ID
- Birth certificate (attested/translated if issued abroad, per some operators)
- Vaccination/immunisation record (UAE National Immunisation Programme card)
- Medical/health declaration form, allergies, and GP/pediatrician details
- Passport-size photos (often 4–6)
- Parents' passport, visa and Emirates ID copies
- Emergency contacts and authorised pick-up persons
- Registration fee and deposit

Sources: https://alfajrecc.com/blog/nursery-admission-checklist-2026 ; https://lppn.ae/steps-to-apply-for-admission-in-a-dubai-nursery-what-you-need-to-know/

## 5. Standards parents should check

### Staff-to-child ratios, Dubai (KHDA ECC guidelines, Jan 2022)
| Age group | Max children per staff |
|---|---|
| 45 days – 17 months | 1:3 |
| 18 – 35 months | 1:5 |
| 36 – 47 months | 1:8 |
| 48 – 71 months | 1:12 |

Sources: https://educationmiddleeast.com/news/khda-announces-strict-guidelines-for-early-childhood-centres-staff/ ; Gulf News 25 Jan 2022 (confirms 1:3 and 1:12). Status: **[MEDIA]**. The KHDA guide itself was not fetched. Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and MoE ratios were **not verified** ([UNCERTAIN]); one secondary source repeats 1:3 for babies in Abu Dhabi.

### Qualifications, Dubai (KHDA 2022) [MEDIA]
- **Centre manager:** bachelor's degree (preferably with a postgraduate early childhood qualification) or a Level 5 vocational qualification in leading an early learning centre; at least 3 years' early childhood experience; KHDA recognition before appointment.
- **Teacher:** bachelor's degree (preferably in ECE) or a Level 3 vocational ECE qualification. For ages 36–71 months, staff should have, or be working towards, an ECE bachelor's degree or a professional ECE certification.
- **Teaching assistant:** a degree, or a high-school certificate plus early childhood experience.
- **Babies and toddlers (45 days–35 months):** staff need expertise in baby and toddler care.
- Background checks and health screening for all staff, per a secondary source [UNCERTAIN].

### Abu Dhabi licence conditions (ADEK, 2019) [OFFICIAL]
- Qualified, licensed principal and staff
- ADEK occupational health and safety compliance (capacity, safety, hygiene)
- Updated medical records
- ADEK-approved fees
- **CCTV monitoring** and emergency protocols
- **Licence displayed in reception**, renewed annually, with renewal applied for at least 2 months before expiry

Parents can check nurseries on the **TAMM** directory, which shows fees, curriculum and **Irtiqaa** inspection ratings [MEDIA, Gulf News 2024].

### Other points
- **Inspection/quality:** Dubai has the ECCE Quality Framework (Nov 2025), a self-review tool; published KHDA nursery **ratings do not exist yet** [MEDIA]. Abu Dhabi has Irtiqaa ratings [MEDIA]. MoE uses a nursery inspection form (2025-26) [OFFICIAL, content not reviewed].
- **Safeguarding law:** Federal Law No. 3 of 2016 (Wadeema's Law, Child Rights) applies to all settings; KHDA cites it [OFFICIAL]. Inclusion law: Federal Law 29/2006 [OFFICIAL, cited in the KHDA FAQ]. Federal Decree-Law 51/2022 requires non-discriminatory admission and support for children with special needs [OFFICIAL, snippet].
- **Health and safety (Dubai):** Dubai Health Authority and Dubai Municipality overlap on health, food and hygiene [UNCERTAIN, secondary]. Specific first-aid or nurse requirements were **not verified**.
- **Parent checklist** (editorial, not regulatory): valid licence on display; ratios by room; staff qualifications and paediatric first aid; CCTV and parent-access policy; safeguarding lead; allergy and medication policy; illness exclusion policy; outdoor shade (KHDA requires shaded, hazard-free outdoor space [MEDIA]); Arabic provision (Dubai).

## 6. Calendar

**Unified UAE academic calendar**, approved Feb 2026 for 2026-27 to 2028-29. It applies to public schools and most private schools. It **excludes** Indian, Pakistani and Bangladeshi curriculum schools (April-start). **Sharjah private schools follow a different schedule.** Private non-MoE schools may split the mid-term break across October and February (max 5 school days total); this option does not apply in Sharjah.

**Nurseries vs schools.** Standalone nurseries often operate close to year-round and run holiday or summer camps rather than following school terms. This is operator practice and not a regulation [UNCERTAIN]. School FS1/FS2 classes follow the school calendar.

## 9. Child development milestones (brief)

- **CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early."** checklists exist for **2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 months, 2 years, 30 months, 3, 4 and 5 years**. In the **2022 revision** (Zubler et al., *Pediatrics*, Feb 2022) the checklists moved to milestones that **≥75%** of children reach by each age, up from the 50th percentile, and **15- and 30-month** checklists were added. Sources: https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/index.html ; https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/149/3/e2021052138/184748/Evidence-Informed-Milestones-for-Developmental. Status: [OFFICIAL].
- Examples from the CDC page: 12m waves bye-bye and pulls up / takes steps; 18m scribbles; 2y kicks a ball; 30m pretend play (e.g. feeding a doll); 3y feeds self with a spoon/fork; 4y draws; 5y increased coordination and social play. Status: [OFFICIAL], summarised; quote exact CDC wording when publishing.
- AAP recommends **developmental screening at 9, 18 and 30 months**, and **autism screening at 18 and 24 months**. Source: the CDC page. Status: [OFFICIAL].
- **WHO Motor Development Study (2006):** there are windows of achievement for 6 gross-motor milestones. For example, **walking alone** ranges from **8.2 to 17.6 months** (1st–99th percentile). Source: https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/child-growth/child-growth-standards/indicators/motor-development-milestones/who-motor-development-study-windows-of-achievement-for-six-gross-motor-development-milestones.pdf. Status: [OFFICIAL].
- **Copy guidance.** Present milestones as guides, not diagnostics, and add "talk to your paediatrician if concerned".
